# CineLog Privacy Policy

Effective date: 2026-10-05

CineLog is a personal movie diary app. This policy explains what data the app
handles. Your library stays on your device; search queries are sent to the
third-party services described below.

## Data handling

CineLog has no accounts, analytics, crash reporting, or developer-operated
servers. The developer does not collect or receive your library or personal
information.

## Data stored on your device

- Your movie library (titles, ratings, reviews, watchlist) in a local database.
- App preferences (onboarding state, reminder schedules).
- Cached movie posters for offline display.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this.

## Network use

When you search for movie details, posters, upcoming releases, or streaming
provider logos, CineLog sends the title you entered to public third-party APIs
(TMDB — The Movie Database, Wikipedia, and the iTunes Search API) to fetch
results. Those providers receive the request and may process connection data
such as your IP address under their own privacy policies:

- TMDB: https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy
- Apple (iTunes Search API): https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Wikimedia (Wikipedia): https://foundation.wikimedia.org/wiki/Policy:Privacy_policy

Your library, ratings, and reviews are not sent to these services.

## Backups

Android's automatic cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for
CineLog. Export creates a JSON file you choose where to save (including a cloud
drive you choose, at your discretion). Import reads a file you pick. CineLog
does not upload or retain exported backup files.

## Permissions

- Internet access — to fetch movie data and images.
- Notifications — only for release reminders you explicitly enable, scheduled
  on-device. No push messaging, no remote notifications.
- Boot completion — only to re-schedule your reminders after a restart.
